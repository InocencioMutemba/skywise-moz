Weather risk, explained for every farmer in Mozambique.

SkyWise Moz turns NASA Earth observation data into a simple 7-day alert: expect floods, expect drought, or plant as planned. Built by MozTech Innovators for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

The challenge

We answer the 2026 challenge “Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data”, which asks teams to use NASA data for climate-resilient agriculture. Our focus is Mozambique, which is hit by cyclones, floods and droughts. Smallholder farmers rarely get clear, local warnings in time to protect crops and seed.

  • Most forecasts are technical, in English, and built for experts.
  • Many farmers rely on a basic phone and low data.
  • Late warnings mean lost harvests and lost income.

How it works

1. Collect

Open NASA datasets for rainfall, temperature and soil moisture are pulled for each province.

2. Score

Simple rules flag flood watch (heavy rain) or drought watch (low rain and dry soil).

3. Advise

Each result becomes one plain-language tip, ready for a light web page or an SMS.

The site is a static page of about 15 KB. It loads fast on slow mobile networks and uses no images or external libraries.

NASA data

  • NASA POWER: daily rainfall and temperature for any point on Earth.
  • GPM IMERG: satellite rainfall estimates.
  • SMAP: soil moisture, to tell dry spells from normal weather.
  • NASA Earthdata: earthdata.nasa.gov for access and documentation.
